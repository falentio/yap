# Equipment-Free Home Workout — A Ladder for Every Pattern

## Goal

Build real strength, muscle, and work capacity with **nothing but floor, a wall, a
doorframe, and the furniture already in the room**. No bar, no bands, no dumbbells.

Every movement pattern gets a ladder you can climb: push (horizontal and
vertical), squat, hinge, pull, core, calves, arms. You enter each ladder at the
rung you can hold for clean reps and only advance when the rung's exit target is
met, so progression never depends on new equipment.

## Prerequisites

- **Three metres of clear floor**, one wall, one doorframe (a closed door works),
  and a table or bed edge at knee-to-hip height.
- **2–3 sessions a week, 30–45 minutes**, on non-consecutive days. Two or three
  weekly sessions produce the most size and strength compared with fewer or more
  ([Harvard Health](https://www.health.harvard.edu/healthy-aging-and-longevity/resistance-training-by-the-numbers)).
- **A chair and a towel** — furniture counts, it is not equipment.
- Cleared for exercise. See a GP first if you have a shoulder, wrist, knee, or
  lower-back injury ([Health](https://www.health.com/how-long-should-you-be-able-to-hold-a-dead-hang-11983865) on the hang, [Marathon Handbook](https://marathonhandbook.com/good-hang-time/) on load tolerance).

Slippers off. On a smooth floor, socks are the tool for the sliding movements
below.

## Steps

### Step 1 — Warm-up (5 minutes)

March in place, leg swings, arm circles, 10 slow good mornings with hands on
hips, 10 slow bodyweight squats, then one easy set of the first rung of your push
and squat ladders ([Nerd Fitness](https://www.nerdfitness.com/blog/beginner-body-weight-workout-burn-fat-build-muscle/)).

### Step 2 — Pick your rung on each ladder

Enter at the highest rung where you can do all prescribed reps with clean form.
**Exit target = advance.** Sets are 3 × 5–8 except where marked; a set ends 1–2
reps short of failure.

**Horizontal push (chest, triceps, front delts)**

| # | Rung | Exit target |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wall push-up, hands at chest height | 3 × 12 |
| 2 | Counter- or table-edge push-up, hands elevated | 3 × 10 |
| 3 | Knee push-up | 3 × 12 |
| 4 | Full push-up, 3 s down | 3 × 10 |
| 5 | Feet-elevated push-up (feet on a chair) | 3 × 8 |
| 6 | Archer push-up (one arm straight, weight to one side) | 3 × 6 per side |
| 7 | One-arm push-up negative | 3 × 5 per side |

Ladder from [Berg Movement](https://www.bergmovement.com/calisthenics-blog/10-best-bodyweight-strength-exercises-no-equipment)
and [Nerd Fitness](https://www.nerdfitness.com/blog/beginner-body-weight-workout-burn-fat-build-muscle/).

**Vertical push (shoulders)**

| # | Rung | Exit target |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pike push-up, hands on floor, hips high | 3 × 6 |
| 2 | Pike push-up, feet on a chair | 3 × 8 |
| 3 | Wall handstand hold, chest to wall | 3 × 30 s |
| 4 | Wall handstand push-up, partial range | 3 × 5 |
| 5 | Wall handstand push-up, full range | 3 × 5 |

Progressions from [Berg Movement](https://www.bergmovement.com/calisthenics-blog/10-best-bodyweight-strength-exercises-no-equipment).

**Squat (quads, glutes)**

| # | Rung | Exit target |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sit-to-stand from a chair, hands on thighs | 3 × 10 |
| 2 | Bodyweight squat, hips below knees, heels down | 3 × 15 |
| 3 | Split squat, rear foot on floor | 3 × 8 per side |
| 4 | Bulgarian split squat, rear foot on a chair | 3 × 8 per side |
| 5 | Assisted pistol squat, holding the doorframe | 3 × 5 per side |
| 6 | Pistol squat to a chair seat, then free | 3 × 5 per side |

Sit-to-stand and squat rungs: [Nerd Fitness](https://www.nerdfitness.com/blog/beginner-body-weight-workout-burn-fat-build-muscle/);
pistol ladder: [Berg Movement](https://www.bergmovement.com/calisthenics-blog/10-best-bodyweight-strength-exercises-no-equipment).

**Hinge (hamstrings, glutes, lower back)**

| # | Rung | Exit target |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Glute bridge, 2 s squeeze at top | 3 × 12 |
| 2 | Single-leg glute bridge | 3 × 10 per side |
| 3 | Bodyweight good morning, hands behind head | 3 × 12 |
| 4 | Single-leg Romanian deadlift, slow | 3 × 8 per side |
| 5 | Sliding leg curl, heels on a towel, hips lifted | 3 × 8 |
| 6 | Nordic curl negative off a sofa edge, hands to catch | 3 × 5 |

Cues from [Fitness Drum](https://fitnessdrum.com/hamstring-exercises-at-home-no-equipment/); the hinge is
the pattern bodyweight covers worst, so it gets a slow tempo, not more reps.

**Horizontal pull (lats, mid-back, biceps) — the one that needs furniture**

| # | Rung | Exit target |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Doorway row: grip both sides of the doorframe, lean back, pull chest through | 3 × 8 |
| 2 | Under-table row, body angled steep, feet close | 3 × 10 |
| 3 | Under-table row, body flat, heels on floor | 3 × 10 |
| 4 | Under-table row, feet elevated on a chair | 3 × 8 |
| 5 | Under-table row, one arm | 3 × 6 per side |
| 6 | Back widow (face up, elbows on floor, press upper back off the floor) | 3 × 8 |

Doorway row and under-table row: [Nerd Fitness](https://www.nerdfitness.com/blog/beginner-body-weight-workout-burn-fat-build-muscle/)
and the [r/bodyweightfitness thread on training without a bar](https://www.reddit.com/r/bodyweightfitness/comments/17mhvrl/how_to_build_an_effective_workout_without_a_pull/).
Back widow: [ATHLEAN-X](https://learn.athleanx.com/articles/back-for-men/bodyweight-back-workout).

**Lat isolation without a bar**

Sliding lat pulldown: lie face down on a smooth floor in socks, arms overhead on
the floor, pull your chest up toward your hands and lower under control. Knees
dragging is the easy version; staying on your toes is the hard one
([ATHLEAN-X](https://learn.athleanx.com/articles/back-for-men/bodyweight-back-workout)).

**Core — anti-extension**

| # | Rung | Exit target |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Knee plank | 3 × 30 s |
| 2 | Full plank | 3 × 45 s |
| 3 | Plank with alternating shoulder tap | 3 × 10 per side |
| 4 | Body saw, feet on a towel | 3 × 8 |
| 5 | Walk-out from standing to plank and back | 3 × 6 |
| 6 | Sliding rollout, hands on a towel | 3 × 8 |

Walk-outs as the no-bar ab rollout: [Berg Movement](https://www.bergmovement.com/calisthenics-blog/10-best-bodyweight-strength-exercises-no-equipment);
knee plank through plank: [Nerd Fitness](https://www.nerdfitness.com/blog/beginner-body-weight-workout-burn-fat-build-muscle/).

**Core — flexion and rotation**

Reverse crunch 3 × 12 → hollow body hold 3 × 20 s → side plank 3 × 30 s per side
→ bicycle crunch 3 × 20 → reverse superman 3 × 10 (face down, arms at sides,
lift arms and legs, 2–3 s squeeze; [ATHLEAN-X](https://learn.athleanx.com/articles/back-for-men/bodyweight-back-workout)).

**Calves, arms, and conditioning**

- Calf raise, single leg off a step edge, 3 × 15 per side, 2 s up / 2 s down.
- Triceps: close-grip push-up from knee, 3 × 8; bench dip on a chair seat, 3 × 10.
- Biceps with nothing: doorway curl — grip the frame, lean back, curl your chest
  toward the frame, 3 × 8. Underhand under-table row is the loaded version.
- Conditioning: 30 s jumping jacks / 30 s march, 8 rounds, or 20 minutes brisk
  walking. The CDC floor is 150 minutes of moderate aerobic work plus
  muscle-strengthening on 2+ days a week
  ([CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity-basics/guidelines/adults.html)).

### Step 3 — Session template

Full body, every session, in this order:

```text
Warm-up                                  5 min
A1  Horizontal push   — your rung        3 × 5–8
A2  Horizontal pull   — your rung        3 × 5–8   (paired, 60 s rest)
B1  Squat             — your rung        3 × 5–8
B2  Hinge             — your rung        3 × 5–8
C1  Vertical push     — your rung        3 × 5–8
C2  Anti-extension    — your rung        3 × 20–45 s
D   Calves + arms                        2 × 12 each
```

Alternate A1/A2 and B1/B2 back to back; rest 60 s between the pairs. Total 30–45
minutes. Two sessions covers the whole body twice a week; a third session is
fine if recovery holds ([Harvard Health](https://www.health.harvard.edu/healthy-aging-and-longevity/resistance-training-by-the-numbers)).

### Step 4 — Progression rule

Hit the exit target on all 3 sets → next session, move up one rung on that
ladder only. Add 2–3 s to every eccentric before adding a rep. When a rung's top
exit target is met, make the rung harder rather than longer: elevate the feet,
narrow the base, go single-limb
([Nerd Fitness](https://www.nerdfitness.com/blog/beginner-body-weight-workout-burn-fat-build-muscle/)
lists exactly these four levers — reps, rest, variation, time under tension).

### Step 5 — Cool-down

Two minutes of walking, then 30 s per side of a doorway chest stretch, a
kneeling hip-flexor stretch, and a standing hamstring reach.

## Verification

Re-test every 4 weeks, in one session:

- Push-up rung → the highest rung you can do 3 × 8 on.
- Under-table row → 3 × 10 at flat body, heels down.
- Squat → 3 × 8 per side split squat.
- Plank → 45 s with no hip sag.
- Note the rung number per pattern in a log.

Progress across four weeks is the signal. Also watch for: no next-day soreness
that limits walking, and no joint pain in the session itself.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| No table to row under | Table too low, legs in the way | Doorway rows, or back widow + sliding lat pulldown |
| Wrists hurt in push-ups | Hands too far forward, no wrist conditioning | Push-up on fists or on knuckles, hands under shoulders |
| Doorframe pulls feel like nothing | Bad angle | Lean further back, feet further from the frame, lower the body more |
| Chair dips hurt the front of the shoulder | Too much depth | Stop at 90° elbow bend, feet closer |
| Knee pain in squats | Hips not travelling back | Sit back to a chair seat, slow the descent, reduce depth |
| Boredom | Only one rung per pattern | Rotate the flexion/rotation core work, alternate split squat and pistol |
| Session runs long | Too many pairs | Drop the D block first, then C2; keep push/pull/squat/hinge |
| Nothing feels hard after 6 weeks | Rungs too easy | Nobody outgrows bodyweight — they outgrow *that rung*. Elevate, narrow, go single-limb |
