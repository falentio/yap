# Gambler × Gamble! (2016) — Gameplay

> **Original edition only.** This file documents the **2016** release (one exclamation mark) by **cosaic / Group SNE**. For the 2024 remake (two exclamation marks), see [Gambler × Gamble!! (2024) — Gameplay](./gambler-x-gamble-2024-remake-gameplay.md).

## TL;DR

- **Gambler × Gamble!** (ギャンブラー×ギャンブル！) is a small Japanese **bluffing / simultaneous-selection card game** for **3–4 players, 15–30 min**, designed by **Kaya Miyano** (宮野華也) with art by **Geco Hirasawa** (平沢下戸). It won the **first Group SNE Board/Card Game Contest (2015)** and was published by **cosaic / Group SNE** on **2016-02-20** ([Group SNE](https://www.groupsne.co.jp/products/bg/gamble/gamble_howto.html), [cosaic](http://www.cosaic.co.jp/games/gg.html)).
- **You hire "gamblers"** (cards with a target number 1–9, a cost, and a reward), then each round **everyone secretly plays a "Destiny" number card**; the **sum of all played numbers** determines which gamblers pay out. Reviewers describe it as a card version of the Japanese **"thumb-guessing" (いっせーの / 指スマ)** game ([deepdepths](https://deepdepths.doorblog.jp/archives/14078908.html), [bodoge-hoobby review](https://bodoge.hoobby.net/games/gambler-x-gamble/reviews/396)).
- **Win either by reaching 15 money chips, or by owning the unique No.4 gambler and having it hit** (its Lucky-Mode face is **No.8**) — an instant-win jackpot card ([Group SNE](https://www.groupsne.co.jp/products/bg/gamble/gamble_howto.html), [bodoge-tokyo](https://bodoge-tokyo.com/games/gambler-gamble)).
- **Signature mechanic — the "Gambler Fumble":** when a round produces **no winner**, the richest player(s) surrender **half their chips** to a pot that is later split among everyone, punishing hoarding ([Group SNE](https://www.groupsne.co.jp/products/bg/gamble/gamble_howto.html), [uramako](https://uramako2.jugem.jp/?eid=739)).
- **Confidence: medium.** The loop is corroborated by the publisher's own tutorial page plus several independent Japanese rule write-ups; the exact **per-card reward values** and a couple of setup details remain unconfirmed (see Open Questions).

### Components

- **Destiny cards (運命カード) — 14:** "0", "1", "2" ×4 each; "3" ×2. *(These are reusable hand cards, not a draw deck.)*
- **Gambler cards (ギャンブラーカード) — 24:** No.1 / No.2 / No.3 ×5 each; **No.4/8 ×1**; No.5 / No.6 / No.7 / No.9 ×2 each. Each card shows a **target number** (top-right), a **reward** (bottom-left), a **cost** (bottom-right), and possibly a **GET icon**.
- **Start-player card ×1** (its two faces read **+1** and **−1**).
- **Fumble-stock card (ファンブルストック) ×1**.
- **Money chips ×28:** value-1 ×20, value-5 ×8.
- 1 rulebook.

([boardgamespeednews](https://boardgamespeednews.seesaa.net/article/449549456.html), [uramako](https://uramako2.jugem.jp/?eid=739), [Group SNE](https://www.groupsne.co.jp/products/bg/gamble/gamble_howto.html))

## Setup

1. **Sort** the gambler cards by target number into a supply. Put the money chips and the "2"/"3" Destiny cards within reach.
2. **Deal each player Destiny "0" and "1"** — the starting hand.
3. **Starting money by seat:** 1st player **1 chip**, 2nd player **1 chip**, 3rd player **2 chips**, 4th player **2 chips** ([uramako](https://uramako2.jugem.jp/?eid=739)). *(The publisher's own summary simplifies this to "two coins each"; see Open Questions.)*
4. **Draft starting gamblers:** each player takes **2 gambler cards from No.1–3**, **free**, in seat order (snake draft — the last player takes 2, then it goes back). At setup only, **duplicate numbers may not be chosen** ([Group SNE](https://www.groupsne.co.jp/products/bg/gamble/gamble_howto.html), [uramako](https://uramako2.jugem.jp/?eid=739)).
5. Choose a start player (e.g. whoever "looks most like a gambler", else janken).

## The round loop

A game is a series of rounds; the start-player card rotates **clockwise** each round. Each round:

1. **Start player plays a Destiny card face-up.**
2. **Everyone else plays one Destiny card face-down.** Players may freely *say* what they played; **showing it is not allowed** ([Group SNE](https://www.groupsne.co.jp/products/bg/gamble/gamble_howto.html), [uramako](https://uramako2.jugem.jp/?eid=739)).
3. **Start player may pay 1 chip to nudge the total ±1** — placing the chip on the +1 or −1 face of the start-player card. **Start-player-only** privilege. If used, the total **must** be adjusted; declining is allowed; only one chip, once per round ([Group SNE](https://www.groupsne.co.jp/products/bg/gamble/gamble_howto.html), [uramako](https://uramako2.jugem.jp/?eid=739)).
4. **Reveal all Destiny cards and total the numbers** (then apply the ±1). Every gambler whose **target number equals the total** is a **winning gambler (当たり)**. **Every** player owning a matching gambler — including duplicates — collects that card's **reward** ([handman](http://handmanboardgame.com/ボードゲーム紹介/ギャンブラー✕ギャンブル！/), [Group SNE](https://www.groupsne.co.jp/products/bg/gamble/gamble_howto.html)).
5. **Start player only: hire a gambler OR enter Lucky Mode** — exactly one.
6. **Cleanup:** return played Destiny cards to hands; pass the start-player card clockwise.

([handman](http://handmanboardgame.com/ボードゲーム紹介/ギャンブラー✕ギャンブル！/), [uramako](https://uramako2.jugem.jp/?eid=739))

### The Gambler Fumble (ギャンブルファンブル)

If a round produces **no winning gambler at all**, the **player(s) with the most chips** must move **half their chips (rounded down)** onto the **Fumble-stock card**. As soon as the stock holds **at least one chip per player**, it is **split evenly among all players**; any remainder stays on the card ([Group SNE](https://www.groupsne.co.jp/products/bg/gamble/gamble_howto.html), [uramako](https://uramako2.jugem.jp/?eid=739)).

This is the game's central balancing mechanism: a bust round **takes money from whoever is ahead and sprinkles it over the table**, so sitting on a big pile is dangerous and everyone is nudged toward making *someone's* number land ([deepdepths](https://deepdepths.doorblog.jp/archives/14078908.html)).

## Hiring and Lucky Mode

At step 5 the start player does exactly one of:

- **Hire a gambler.** Pay the card's **cost** (bottom-right number) and place it face-up in front of you. Cards marked **"GET 2" / "GET 3"** additionally **grant you the "2" or "3" Destiny card** (usable from the next round). Duplicate gambler numbers can be hired freely, but each of the "2"/"3" Destiny cards can be obtained **only once per player** ([uramako](https://uramako2.jugem.jp/?eid=739), [deepdepths](https://deepdepths.doorblog.jp/archives/14078908.html)).
- **Enter Lucky Mode (豪運モード).** Flip **all** of your gamblers of **one chosen number** to their reverse side, **free**. Lucky gamblers pay **more** when they hit, but a Lucky gambler **reverts to normal after it wins once** ([Group SNE](https://www.groupsne.co.jp/products/bg/gamble/gamble_howto.html), [handman](http://handmanboardgame.com/ボードゲーム紹介/ギャンブラー✕ギャンブル！/)).

**Cost / reward economics:**

| Gambler | Cost | Grants | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| No.1, No.2, No.3 | 1 | — | Starting pool; cheap, low reward |
| No.5, No.6, No.7 | 3 | Destiny "2" | Mid-tier; widens your reach |
| No.9 | 3 | Destiny "3" | High number; nearly dead at 3 players |
| **No.4 / No.8** | **10** | — | **Instant-win lottery card** (No.4 normal, No.8 Lucky) |

([deepdepths](https://deepdepths.doorblog.jp/archives/14078908.html), [uramako](https://uramako2.jugem.jp/?eid=739))

Because higher-numbered gamblers pay more, the usual arc is to **buy bigger numbers as the Destiny pool grows**; hiring **duplicates of a number you already own multiplies that number's payout** ([chokobodo](https://chokobodo.com/gambler-x-gamble/), [deepdepths](https://deepdepths.doorblog.jp/archives/14078908.html)).

## Win conditions

The game ends the moment any of these is true:

1. **Chip race:** a player holds **15 or more chips** ([bodoge-tokyo](https://bodoge-tokyo.com/games/gambler-gamble), [uramako](https://uramako2.jugem.jp/?eid=739)).
2. **The No.4 jackpot:** a player owning the **No.4 gambler** has it be a **winning gambler** — they win instantly. If that player has flipped the No.4 card to its **Lucky (No.8)** face, they win on a hit of **8** instead of 4. The No.4/8 card is **unique (1 copy)**, first-come first-served ([Group SNE](https://www.groupsne.co.jp/products/bg/gamble/gamble_howto.html), [bodoge-tokyo](https://bodoge-tokyo.com/games/gambler-gamble)).

**Ties:** if chip totals are equal, the player holding **fewer gambler cards** wins; if still tied, the victory is shared ([uramako](https://uramako2.jugem.jp/?eid=739)).

## Strategy and player interaction

- **The ±1 is decisive.** Many rounds hinge on whether the start player nudges the sum onto or off a target; 1 chip is trivial next to what it changes ([chokobodo](https://chokobodo.com/gambler-x-gamble/), [bodoge-hoobby review](https://bodoge.hoobby.net/games/gambler-x-gamble/reviews/396)).
- **Table talk is the game.** Only the start player plays face-up, so every other card is a *claim*; lying is explicitly allowed, and reading opponents' incentives (which numbers *they* own) matters more than the cards ([Group SNE](https://www.groupsne.co.jp/products/bg/gamble/gamble_howto.html)).
- **Collusion and sabotage are legal.** Players may coordinate to force a bust and drain the chip leader via the Fumble ([handman](http://handmanboardgame.com/ボードゲーム紹介/ギャンブラー✕ギャンブル！/)).
- **Wide vs. tall:** buying many different numbers widens your hit range (steady income); stacking duplicates of one number and flipping it to Lucky Mode is the high-variance "one big score" line ([chokobodo](https://chokobodo.com/gambler-x-gamble/), [deepdepths](https://deepdepths.doorblog.jp/archives/14078908.html)).
- **No.9 is nearly dead in 3-player** — a sum of 9 needs most players to play high *and* the start player to add +1 ([bodoge-hoobby](https://bodoge.hoobby.net/games/gambler-x-gamble), [chokobodo](https://chokobodo.com/gambler-x-gamble/)).
- **No.4 is trap-or-jackpot:** spending 10 chips to buy it badly delays the 15-chip race; reviewers often conclude it is better to keep earning ([deepdepths](https://deepdepths.doorblog.jp/archives/14078908.html), [chokobodo](https://chokobodo.com/gambler-x-gamble/)).

## Reception

- **BGG ratings conflict by snapshot.** A 2017-03 snapshot lists **7.2/10** ([handman](http://handmanboardgame.com/ボードゲーム紹介/ギャンブラー✕ギャンブル！/)); a later aggregator shows **5.6/10, rank ≈11,700, weight 1.33** ([boardgamesforallages](https://boardgamesforallages.com/game/gambler-x-gamble/)). Both are point-in-time and **not directly comparable**.
- **Play-feel votes (bodoge-hoobby)** skew heavily to **psychology/bluff (13)** and **strategy (11)** over **luck/probability (3)** — players read it as a reading game, not a dice game ([bodoge-hoobby](https://bodoge.hoobby.net/games/gambler-x-gamble)).
- **Praise:** quick, cheap, elegant "reading" filler; nice art; the Lucky-Mode art flip is a favourite detail. **Criticism:** "a bit coarse/large-grained", 3-player balance issues, and the No.4 cost being questionable ([bodoge-hoobby reviews](https://bodoge.hoobby.net/games/gambler-x-gamble), [chokobodo](https://chokobodo.com/gambler-x-gamble/)).

## Open Questions

- **Starting chips.** Official summary says "two coins"; a detailed Japanese walkthrough gives a seat-based **1/1/2/2** split ([uramako](https://uramako2.jugem.jp/?eid=739)). Which is the shipped rule is unresolved.
- **±1 chip destination.** One source says the paid chip goes to the **supply**; whether the Fumble stock or bank is meant is not pinned down.
- **Full gambler card table.** Per-card target/cost/reward/lucky-reward values were not captured (card images were not readable). Costs 1/3/10 and the GET-2/GET-3 rules are attested; the rest of the table is not.
- **Lucky Mode payout multiplier.** Confirmed to be "higher", exact numbers unknown.
- **English rulebook.** No official English rules PDF found; English coverage is secondary/derivative.
- **BGG page bot-blocked.** [Entry 197071](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/197071/gambler-gamble) returned `bot_blocked` on 2026-10-11; rating/rank/mechanic tags not captured directly.
