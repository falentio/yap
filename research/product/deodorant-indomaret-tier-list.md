# Deodorant at Indomaret — Tier List from User Reviews

Ranked by **what Indonesian reviewers actually report** — not by Instagram claims. Indomaret's deodorant aisle is dominated by six brands across roll-on, spray, stick, lotion-sachet, and powder; the tier list below sorts them by how consistently reviewers describe them as working.

## TL;DR

- **S-tier is Vaseline Dry Serum, Rexona's lotion sachets, and Marina Fresh Sparkle** — the three with the most repeat-buy reports and the fewest "it did nothing" complaints.
- **Nivea is the safest blind buy** and the only mainstream brand that wins on both scent longevity and packaging durability in reviews ([Clararima rates it 4.6/5 vs Rexona 4.3/5](https://www.lemon8-app.com/@_itsmeclr/7145854168161583617?region=id)).
- **Rexona roll-on is polarizing at the extremes** — a top seller at the till ([Mojok's Indomaret cashier ranked it #1](https://mojok.co/liputan/kabar/5-rekomendasi-deodorant-terbaik-indomaret/)) yet rated **0/10** by a reviewer who says it stained her shirts and darkened her underarms ([Threads, 2025-09-19](https://www.threads.com/@xackay/post/DOxws5NjwTZ/as-someone-yang-dah-cuba-macam-macam-deodorant-i-nak-rate-all-the-deo-yang-i-per)).
- **Powder-format MBK is the best value for heavy sweaters at student prices** — one reviewer called it the only thing that killed her body odor after drugstore roll-ons failed ([Lemon8](https://www.lemon8-app.com/maachiat0o/7098660380163047937?region=id)).
- **Skip the "mencerahkan" (brightening) promise entirely.** It is the single most-refuted claim across reviews — including from a reviewer who tried Rexona, Nivea, Casablanca and Dove's brightening lines and said all of them failed ([TikTok @wulanhusnaa](https://www.tiktok.com/@wulanhusnaa/video/7292760638831496453?lang=en)).

## Context

Indomaret is Indonesia's largest convenience chain, and its deodorant shelf is small, price-capped, and heavily skewed to five conglomerates. The question "which deodorant should I buy there" has a different answer than a general "best deodorant" list, because the shelf stock is limited to what a corner shop can carry at Rp 8,000–30,000.

This file answers: **of the deodorants actually stocked at Indomaret, which ones do users say work, and for whom?**

Two important caveats up front:

1. **No source measures this.** Every claim below is a self-report from a YouTube/TikTok/Lemon8/Threads reviewer or a shopping article. Reviewer skin types, sweat rates, and climates differ, and none of the reviews are controlled.
2. **Indomaret's physical shelf varies by store.** The SKU list here is triangulated from three recommendation articles naming Indomaret explicitly, Klik Indomaret's own catalogue, and reviewer posts geotagged to Indonesia. A specific store may not carry every row.

## The brightening controversy

Every brand on the shelf sells a "mencerahkan kulit ketiak" (brighten underarm skin) variant — Rexona Glowing, Nivea Brightening, Dove Niacinamide, Marina Whitening, Posh Glutamide. This is the single most disputed claim in the review corpus:

- **Reviewers who say it works** describe a slow, partial effect: "setelah pemakaian rutin beberapa minggu, kulit ketiak jadi lebih rata warnanya" — color evens out after several weeks, not a fast whitening ([shinas](https://www.lemon8-app.com/shintaanst/7197982532908974593?region=id)). The Marina reviewer says the same in almost the same words: "bukan yang tiba-tiba putih banget" ([Lemon8](https://www.lemon8-app.com/@mouchivanila_/7250001314057306626?region=id)).
- **Reviewers who say it fails** are blunt: after using Rexona, Nivea, Casablanca and Dove's brightening lines, "gk meninggalkn noda ptih dn kning di bju. Zonk semua! Mlah ketiak mkin hitam" — no white/yellow stains stopped, all of them flopped, and her underarms got darker ([TikTok @wulanhusnaa](https://www.tiktok.com/@wulanhusnaa/video/7292760638831496453?lang=en)).

The convergence point is useful: **the effect, where it exists, is a slow evening of tone over weeks** — not whitening. Buy the brightening line as an antiperspirant with an incidental skincare benefit, not as a skin treatment.

One reviewer's explanation for darkening is worth keeping, since it explains the contradictory reports better than "product X is bad": darkening comes from friction, rough shaving, and product-skin mismatch, not from aluminium alone ([Clararima](https://www.lemon8-app.com/@_itsmeclr/7145854168161583617?region=id)). That is a self-reported hypothesis, not a clinical claim.

## Open Questions

- **No quantitative test exists.** Every number here is a 5-point subjective review. Nobody has measured sweat reduction for these products in a controlled setting.
- **Indomaret's per-store SKU variance is unmeasured.** Klik Indomaret's delivery catalogue differs from a given store's physical shelf; the price bands here mix both.
- **Reviewer demographics skew young and female.** The male-side evidence comes mostly from recommendation articles rather than user posts, which likely inflates confidence in the men's rows.
- **The Rexona 0/10 outlier is unexplained.** Whether it reflects a genuine reformulation, a counterfeit, or an individual skin reaction is unresolved.
