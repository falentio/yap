# Gambler × Gamble! — Which Edition Is Better? (User Reviews, 2016 vs 2024)

> Companion to the two rules files: [Gambler × Gamble! (2016)](./gambler-x-gamble-2016-gameplay.md) and [Gambler × Gamble!! (2024 remake)](./gambler-x-gamble-2024-remake-gameplay.md). This file is only about **reception and the "which one" question.**

## TL;DR — the verdict

- **There is no settled consensus, and the two editions are not equally reviewed.** The **2016 original** is the proven, widely-played edition: **~168–169 BGG ratings** (avg ≈**6.6**, Bayesian "Geek Rating" ≈**5.6**, rank ≈**11,660–11,700**) and **10–11 Japanese reviews** on bodoge-hoobby. The **2024 remake** has only **~19 BGG ratings and 6–7 comments** and **0 reviews** on bodoge-hoobby — so any "better" verdict is thin ([BGG 197071](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/197071/gambler-gamble/ratings), [BGG 436522](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/436522/gambler-gamble/ratings), [bodoge-hoobby](https://bodoge.hoobby.net/games/gambler-x-gamble), [bodoge-hoobby remake](https://bodoge.hoobby.net/games/gambler-gamble)).
- **If you want the safer, better-validated game: the 2016 original.** It has the volume of positive reviews, the award pedigree, and years of "always well received" word-of-mouth ([bodoge-hoobby review](https://bodoge.hoobby.net/games/gambler-x-gamble/reviews/41039)).
- **If you are bothered by the original's #1 complaint — first-player advantage — the 2024 remake is the fix.** Its single most detailed user comment says the remake **lets everyone buy each turn, not just the lead player**, and reduces the fiddly fumble bookkeeping ([BGG 436522 comment](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/436522/gambler-gamble/ratings?rated=1&comment=1)).
- **But that same review says the remake is "much more loose" with "fewer reasons for players to plot with each other."** Since bluffing/plotting *is* the point of the original, this is the key trade-off: the remake is **less sharp but more even and less fiddly** ([BGG 436522 comment](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/436522/gambler-gamble/ratings?rated=1&comment=1)).
- **One-line recommendation:** for a bluffing group that already knows the original, the remake is optional; for a new buyer who dislikes first-player snowballing, the remake is the more forgiving entry. Neither is a clear "upgrade everything" winner.

## What users say about the 2016 original

### Positive

- **"This is a *lot* of fun! There's lots to think about when trying to figure out which bidding card players might go with and shared incentives develop as the game goes on."** — rated 7, BGG user, Sept 2022 ([BGG 197071 comments](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/197071/gambler-gamble/ratings?comment=1), [geeklist](https://boardgamegeek.com/geeklist/304585/new-to-you-september-2022-equal-best-new-boardgame)).
- **"This is a quick and easy gambling and outguessing game."** — BGG user ([BGG 197071 comments](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/197071/gambler-gamble/ratings?comment=1)).
- **"A filler rating for a light random gambling game. Lots and lots of laughs … when someone loses half their money."** — i.e. the Fumble *is* the fun ([BGG 197071 comments](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/197071/gambler-gamble/ratings?comment=1)).
- **"Strong bluffing + betting game…"** — BGG user ([BGG 197071](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/197071/gambler-gamble/ratings?comment=1)).
- Japanese reviewer (たつきち, 2023): *"a light good game… 30 minutes but fairly thoughtful… recommended as an out-bluffing game 💮"*, and notes that **once someone buys the No.4 card the table tenses up** ([bodoge-hoobby review 41039](https://bodoge.hoobby.net/games/gambler-x-gamble/reviews/41039)).
- Japanese reviewer (こいち): *"simple rules, but the [Fumble] and the start player's ±1 for 1 coin work really well and make for good jockeying"* ([bodoge-hoobby review 396](https://bodoge.hoobby.net/games/gambler-x-gamble/reviews/396)).
- Japanese reviewer (あまとう): *"a roulette-like card game where you guess the field total… easy psychological battle"* ([bodoge-hoobby review 3896](https://bodoge.hoobby.net/games/gambler-x-gamble/reviews/3896)).
- Yodobashi buyer (owned ~9 years): *"watched a play video and bought it — it's probably quite a bit better than I expected; if you like bluffing and jockeying it gets exciting"* ([Yodobashi](https://www.yodobashi.com/community/product/100000001003011464/review.html)).
- Reviewer (hanikinu): likes the **Lucky-Mode art transformation**, and finds **stacking the same number strong** ([bodoge-hoobby review 2824](https://bodoge.hoobby.net/games/gambler-x-gamble/reviews/2824)).

### Negative / caveats

- **"Nice mechanism, but too much first player advantage. Last player has to play a whole round before buying/flipping, and this game barely lasts 2~3 rounds."** — the most-cited structural complaint, and the one the remake targets ([BGG 197071 comments](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/197071/gambler-gamble/ratings?comment=1)).
- **"A filler rating for a light random gambling game."** — i.e. reviewers agree it is *light and random*, not a thinker's game ([BGG 197071 comments](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/197071/gambler-gamble/ratings?comment=1)).
- **3-player balance:** Japanese reviewer たつきち and the chokobodo round-up both flag that **No.9 is nearly unreachable at 3 players** ([bodoge-hoobby](https://bodoge.hoobby.net/games/gambler-x-gamble), [chokobodo](https://chokobodo.com/gambler-x-gamble/)).
- **"A bit coarse/large-grained"** and **the No.4 card's cost is questionable** — you often do better just racing to 15 ([chokobodo](https://chokobodo.com/gambler-x-gamble/)).
- **Not everyone likes it:** a 5ch board-games thread poster wrote flatly *"ギャンブラーギャンブルはクソつまらんかった"* ("Gambler Gamble was boring as hell") — a reminder that the "always well received" claim is not universal ([5ch 卓上ゲーム thread](https://itest.5ch.net/medaka/test/read.cgi/cgame/1616230748/l-)).

## What users say about the 2024 remake

The remake's reception rests on **one detailed comparison comment** plus the publisher's own pitch.

### The key comparison comment (BGG, verbatim fragments)

> **"Compared to the first edition, it's much more loose, more reasons to deviate from the standard cards, but that also means fewer reasons for players to plot with each other.... openly at least."**

> **"There's more of each card, so it's a little harder to get shut out of a number, but still possible."**

> **"it's a wild gambling game and this makes it even more wild. Anyway, people only conspired to slow down the game, not accomplish anything, so that was probably a red herring to begin with."**

> **"Fewer fumbles to keep track of, but they weren't that exciting. It's GAMBLING, not DISTRIBUTE MONEY FAIRLY, geez."**

> **"One thing I love is everyone gets a buy each turn, not just the lead player, so the game actually…"**

([BGG 436522 comments](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/436522/gambler-gamble/ratings?rated=1&comment=1))

**Decoding it:**

- ✅ **Fixes first-player advantage** — *"everyone gets a buy each turn, not just the lead player."* This directly answers the original's biggest complaint.
- ✅ **Fewer shutouts** — more copies of each card means it is harder to be locked out of a number.
- ✅ **Less bookkeeping** — fewer Fumbles to track.
- ⚠️ **Less plotting** — the trade-off. "More reasons to deviate from the standard cards" reduces the *shared* incentive to scheme, which is exactly the texture fans of the original praised.
- ⚠️ **The Fumble is de-emphasised** — and this commenter disliked that (*"It's GAMBLING, not DISTRIBUTE MONEY FAIRLY"*), even though other players love the Fumble.

### Other remake signals

- A BGG commenter notes the remake has **"ten different gambler cards and their 'super mode' side"** — i.e. a 10th gambler type and the Lucky/"super" mode carried over ([BGG 436522](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/436522/gambler-gamble)).
- One-line BGG comment: **"Great Game!!! Great Game!!! Great Game!!!"** — enthusiastic but uninformative ([BGG 436522](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/436522/gambler-gamble)).
- The only detailed English play report calls it **"more than meets the eye"**, notes **negotiation and deception can play a big part**, and says the reviewer **needs to replay** to judge it ([Hiew's Boardgame Blog, 2026-08-07](https://hiewandboardgames.blogspot.com/2026/08/gambler-x-gamble.html)).
- **Publisher's framing** is that the remake is *flashier and tenser to the end*, with more hiring windows and zero-money comeback gamblers ([Group SNE 2024 page](https://www.groupsne.co.jp/products/bg/gamble/index.html)).
- **Japanese database silence:** bodoge-hoobby's remake page has **0 reviews and 0 strategy posts**, with only ~30 logged plays vs the original's ~535 ([bodoge-hoobby remake](https://bodoge.hoobby.net/games/gambler-gamble)). The Japanese audience has not spoken yet.

## So which is better?

**There is no evidence-based "the remake is strictly better" verdict.** What the evidence supports:

| If you… | Pick | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Want the proven, most-discussed, best-validated version | **2016 original** | 168+ ratings, 10+ reviews, award pedigree, "always well received" word-of-mouth |
| Hate first-player snowballing / the last player waiting a full round | **2024 remake** | "everyone gets a buy each turn, not just the lead player" |
| Want the *sharpest* bluffing/plotting experience | **2016 original** (tentatively) | the remake is "much more loose" with "fewer reasons for players to plot with each other" |
| Want a cleaner, less fiddly Fumble | **2024 remake** | "fewer fumbles to keep track of" |
| Want the Fumble chaos as the main event | **2016 original** | the remake de-emphasises it, to at least one player's displeasure |
| Are buying blind with no strong preference | **2016 original** | more reviews, cheaper on the used market, and the remake's own review base is too small to trust |

**Bottom line:** the remake is a **deliberate rebalance aimed at the original's known weaknesses** (turn-order advantage, fumble bookkeeping, narrow number availability), and by that measure it likely plays *fairer and smoother*. But the strongest comparative review suggests it also **blunts the plotting that made the original distinctive**, and with only ~19 BGG ratings the remake has not yet earned a reputation. The original remains the safer pick; the remake is the pick for someone specifically annoyed by the original's first-player problem.

## Open Questions

- **Remake rating volume.** ~19 BGG ratings and 0 Japanese reviews is far too little for a stable verdict; the comparison should be re-run once the remake has 50+ ratings.
- **BGG pages bot-blocked.** Both ratings pages returned `bot_blocked`; the rating counts, averages, and comment text above come from search snippets, so exact figures may be slightly stale or paraphrased.
- **No head-to-head play test found.** No source does a controlled same-group comparison of both editions; the "better" answer is inferred from complaints each edition fixes or creates.
- **Whether the remake's "more loose" character is widely felt.** One detailed commenter says so; with only 6–7 comments total, this may be one person's read.
- **Remake Japanese reception.** bodoge-hoobby shows ~30 logged plays and no reviews; Japanese blogs/tweets specifically reviewing the remake were not found.
- **Cost.** The remake is ¥2,750 new vs a ¥1,000–1,500 used market for the original (Mercari listings), which matters for the "which should I buy" decision but is not a gameplay judgement.
