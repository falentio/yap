# Power Bulletin! — Base Game and Expansions (Head Office, Back Alley)

## TL;DR

- **Power Bulletin!** is a small, non-collectible **hero-vs-villain card game** from Indonesian indie studio **No Punny Games** (Jakarta), designed by **Abe Mitsu Teru** ("rithgroove"). It released in **2024** and sells mainly through Indonesian doujin events (Comifuro) and local marketplaces ([BGG](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/433366/power-bulletin), [Reroll](https://reroll.id/games/power-bulletin), [LinkedIn](https://id.linkedin.com/in/rithgroove)).
- **2–4 players, ~10–15 min, age 15+, deduction + hand management.** Your **hand is your life**: you win by **eliminating opponents** (stripping cards from their hands) **or by holding the highest total Power when the deck runs out** ([BGG](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/433366/power-bulletin), [Reroll](https://reroll.id/games/power-bulletin)).
- Two expansions exist. **Head Office** (1st, 2024) adds a new **Civilian** faction whose cards **count as both Hero and Villain** and use a new **"On Discard"** trigger. **Back Alley** (2nd, 2026) adds the **Necromancer** and **Underdog** characters and "adds more flexibility and tightens the game" ([Reroll Head Office](https://reroll.id/games/power-bulletin-head-office-expansion), [Reroll Back Alley](https://reroll.id/games/power-bulletin-back-alley-expansion), [Instagram Civilian preview](https://www.instagram.com/p/DWfKBqagWsl/)).
- **Confidence is capped at medium**: the game is Indonesian-only, has no published English rulebook PDF, and the publisher's own website is still a development placeholder. Base-game card text and the exact turn sequence below are reconstructed from the designer's behind-the-scenes post, publisher FAQ comments, and reviewer posts, not a rulebook ([No Punny Games site](https://www.nopunnygames.com/products/power-bulletin), [designer post](https://www.facebook.com/nopunnygames/posts/rithgrooves-rambling-behind-the-scene-of-power-bulletinnow-that-the-preparation-/122144600888052360/)).

## The core idea

From the publisher's own one-line pitch and BGG blurb ([BGG](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/433366/power-bulletin)):

> "Power Bulletin! is a short card game designed to be played with 2 to 4 players for 10 to 15 minutes. The game's goal is to **eliminate opponents by removing cards from their hands** or by **having the most powerful hand once the deck runs out**. Do YOU have the Power?!"

The design signature, stated by the designer in a development diary: **"we know that we wanted the player's hand as their life point"** and **"we decided on the theme hero vs villain since the beginning"** ([designer post](https://www.facebook.com/nopunnygames/posts/rithgrooves-rambling-behind-the-scene-of-power-bulletinnow-that-the-preparation-/122144600888052360/)). So there is no separate health track — losing cards from your hand *is* taking damage.

### Win conditions (two)

1. **Elimination.** Reduce every opponent's hand to nothing (cards are removed/discarded by attacks and powers).
2. **Deck exhaustion.** When the deck runs out, the player with the **highest total Power in hand** wins ([BGG](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/433366/power-bulletin)).

A fan question on the official tutorial post asks exactly how to score a mixed hand at deck-out (e.g. Healer 3 + Guard 1 + Gambler 5). The asker's reading — **sum the Power of *all* cards in hand, Hero and Villain alike** (total 9) — is the natural one, but the publisher had not answered publicly at the time of research, so treat "sum across factions" as **probable, not confirmed** [unverified] ([tutorial post comments](https://www.facebook.com/nopunnygames/posts/folks-for-those-who-want-to-order-but-still-thinking-whether-its-fun-or-not-here/122236708250052360/)).

## Base game: characters and cards

The base game was designed around **10 characters** that interact with one another, **5 Heroes and 5 Villains** ([designer post](https://www.facebook.com/nopunnygames/posts/rithgrooves-rambling-behind-the-scene-of-power-bulletinnow-that-the-preparation-/122144600888052360/)). The designer published the **earliest-stage** versions of these codenames; several were renamed and reworked during playtesting, so the shipped powers differ (notably the Seer):

| Faction | Early codename | Early effect (2013–14 prototype) | Shipped name (if changed) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hero | Attacker | Hits randomly, but damages you if it hits a "good guy" | Attacker |
| Hero | Seer | Look at an opponent's hand | **became a card-draw engine** |
| Hero | Healer | Heals the player | Healer |
| Hero | Anti-Villain | Hits 2 Villains | **Hitman** |
| Hero | Guard | Negates an opponent's attack and damages them | Guard |
| Villain | Attacker | Hits anything | Attacker |
| Villain | Vengeance | Hits everyone | Vengeance |
| Villain | Guesser | Guess to deal damage | **Gambler** |
| Villain | Comparer | Compare Power to deal damage | **Provocateur** |
| Villain | Tactician | Negates an opponent's attack and heals the player | Tactician |

The designer explains the Seer rework: once most cards gained "look at target player's hand" effects, the Seer's original ability was redundant, so it became a draw engine ([designer post](https://www.facebook.com/nopunnygames/posts/rithgrooves-rambling-behind-the-scene-of-power-bulletinnow-that-the-preparation-/122144600888052360/)).

**Cards carry a Power number** used for the deck-exhaustion win check. Confirmed values from a public rules question: **Healer = 3, Guard = 1, Gambler = 5** ([tutorial post comments](https://www.facebook.com/nopunnygames/posts/folks-for-those-who-want-to-order-but-still-thinking-whether-its-fun-or-not-here/122236708250052360/)).

## Base game: turn structure

The rulebook has no public PDF, so the loop below is assembled from the publisher's FAQ answers and the "decide the target on Play Phase" comment. It is a **partial** reconstruction — flag anything you need to be exact on.

The game runs on **named phases** within a turn, at minimum:

1. **Play Phase.** The active player plays a card and **decides its target during this phase** (publisher FAQ: *"Decide the target on Play Phase"*) ([No Punny Games FAQ](https://www.facebook.com/nopunnygames/)).
2. **Reaction Phase.** Opponents may respond with **reaction cards**. Hard cap: **"once a player plays a reaction card, the reaction phase ends. No other reaction card can be played"** ([No Punny Games FAQ](https://www.facebook.com/nopunnygames/)). So a single reaction is allowed per trigger, not a reaction chain.
3. **Discard / On Discard.** Cards are discarded to a **discard pile**, and (in the expansions) some cards have **"On Discard"** abilities that fire only when discarded ([Instagram Civilian preview](https://www.instagram.com/p/DWfKBqagWsl/), [reviewer note on Necromancer](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DdgQsSWpqAq/)).

**Two documented FAQ rulings:**

- *"Can I play another card in reaction phase?"* → **No**; the first reaction card ends the reaction phase ([No Punny Games FAQ](https://www.facebook.com/nopunnygames/)).
- *"Can I play this card as the last card in my hand?"* → **Yes**, because the rule text reads *"if you're able to"*, so the extra cost/placement is not mandatory ([No Punny Games FAQ](https://www.facebook.com/nopunnygames/)).

> **Gap:** the exact draw step, hand-size limits, and the full action menu for the Play Phase are **not** captured by any public source found. The official "How to Play" video exists ([tutorial post](https://www.facebook.com/nopunnygames/posts/folks-for-those-who-want-to-order-but-still-thinking-whether-its-fun-or-not-here/122236708250052360/), [WHATT PLAY playthrough](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XDEuhE2WaxY)) but its transcript was not extractable by text tools. A physical rulebook is the only authoritative source.

## Expansions

The line grew to **base + expansions**, sold as a discounted bundle ("1 Power Bulletin! + 3 Expansion") on pre-order ([Comifuro 23 order form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScjb3VQFE7ggSrTgqFOHZ7R1BtNTcB5iCfOrogmXdQu1MrV1Q/viewform)). Two expansions are documented in the board-game databases: **Head Office** and **Back Alley**.

**What it adds — the Civilian faction.** Head Office is the **first** expansion and introduces a new faction, **Civilian**, with two structural twists ([Instagram Civilian preview](https://www.instagram.com/p/DWfKBqagWsl/)):

- **Civilians count as both Heroes *and* Villains.** ("Kartu civilian terhitung sebagai Heroes dan Villain.") This makes them wild for any effect that cares about faction.
- **New "On Discard" condition.** Civilian cards have an **On Discard** ability that only triggers **when the card is discarded**, not when played.

**New characters.** Head Office introduces **Reporter**, shown by the publisher as a card obtainable from the expansion, with alternate art variants ([Reporter variant post](https://www.instagram.com/p/Dc7j2dkEnZU/)). The designer's prototype notes also list **Reporter** as "a new character on Power Bulletin! 2" ([designer post](https://www.facebook.com/nopunnygames/posts/rithgrooves-rambling-behind-the-scene-of-power-bulletinnow-that-the-preparation-/122144600888052360/)).

> **Gap:** the full Head Office card list and the exact Reporter / Civilian card text are not in any public source found; only the faction rules and the Reporter preview are attested.

**The two headline characters:**

- **Necromancer.** A reviewer's favourite: it "makes things more strategic" because it can **use abilities from the discard pile**, which "breaks the card-count maths" — i.e. it recycles/uses already-discarded cards, undermining the usual hand-count-as-life arithmetic ([reviewer reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DdgQsSWpqAq/)).
- **Underdog.** A card named **Crimson Fang** is shown as the **Underdog** character; its preview text includes a play cost of 1 and a *[Main/Action]* timing block ([Facebook Underdog preview](https://www.facebook.com/nopunnygames/)). The exact effect is not fully captured [unverified].

**Announced via:** a "Announcing Power Bulletin! Back Alley Expansion! Coming Soon!" post on 2026-08-11, then a pre-order (August 2026), with the publisher teasing character variants (**Miho**, **Killa**, **Nightingale the Healer**) ahead of release ([announcement](https://www.facebook.com/nopunnygames/posts/announcing-power-bulletin-back-alley-expansion-coming-soon/122235316460052360/), [variant post](https://www.instagram.com/p/DcM0UyKknW0/)).

> **Gap:** the Back Alley card list is only partially public; the Necromancer and Underdog effects are known only from a reviewer summary and a single card preview. **"Miho / Killa / Nightingale"** appear to be alternate-art variants of existing characters (Nightingale is the Healer), not new characters — treat as probable [unverified] ([variant post](https://www.instagram.com/p/DcM0UyKknW0/)).

## Reviews and reception (what players say)

- **Art is the hook.** Multiple Indonesian reviewers lead with the anime-style art: "ART-NYA KEREN BANGET!" ([Wiwa Boardgame](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DdTcWaNPazU/)) and "Artnya bagus betul" ([reviewer comments](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DdgQsSWpqAq/)).
- **Familiar feel.** A commenter on the official tutorial calls it "love letter banget" (very *Love Letter*-like) — an audience comparison, not a designer claim [unverified] ([tutorial post](https://www.facebook.com/zack.beowolf/posts/tutorial-power-bulletin/10237257214424789/)).
- **Expansions improve it.** A reviewer who played base + both expansions says it "jadi makin soliddd" (became much more solid), singling out the Necromancer as the standout ([reviewer reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DdgQsSWpqAq/)).
- **Popular at the booth.** The publisher reports **160+ units** of Power Bulletin and its expansions sold in a single pre-order window ([publisher post](https://www.instagram.com/p/DdOxoQUGvQ8/)).
