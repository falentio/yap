# Nivea Men Deodorant — Tier List by Variant

Nivea Men is the most-reviewed men's deodorant range in Indonesia, which finally makes a variant-level ranking possible. Ranked by FemaleDaily member ratings, not by packaging claims.

## TL;DR

- **S-tier: Deep Darkwood and Deep Charcoal Espresso.** They are the only variants whose reviews are almost entirely about *sweat control working*, and the numbers back it — Deep Charcoal Espresso at **4.4/5 with 85% recommended**, Deep Darkwood at **4.7/5 with 100% recommended** ([FemaleDaily](https://reviews.femaledaily.com/products/men-s-care/men-s-body-care/nivea/nivea-men-deep-charcoal-espresso-3), [FemaleDaily](https://reviews.femaledaily.com/products/men-s-care/men-s-body-care/nivea/men-roll-on-deodorant-deep-darkwood)).
- **A-tier: Black & White Invisible Original (4.8/5)** — the anti-stain specialist, and the only variant where the *clothes* claim is the thing reviewers actually verify.
- **B-tier: Cool Kick.** The most-bought variant and the one the Indomaret cashiers push, but the lowest-rated here at **4.1/5, 81% recommended**, with a consistent complaint: the cooling is nice and the sweat control isn't ([FemaleDaily](https://reviews.femaledaily.com/products/men-s-care/men-s-body-care/nivea/men-cool-kick-cool-blue-deo-roll-on)).
- **Freezy Green scores a perfect 5.0 but only from 7 reviews** — treat that number as unproven, not as the best variant.
- **Buy by job, not by scent:** Deep = sweat, Black & White = shirt stains, Cool Kick = a brief cooling hit, Fresh Active = light/office wear.

## Context

The men's Indomaret file put **Nivea Men Silver Protect / Black & White** at the top of the men's shelf on the strength of r/indonesia discussion ([deodorant-men-indomaret-tier-list.md](./deodorant-men-indomaret-tier-list.md)). "Nivea Men" is not one product though — the Indonesian site carries roughly a dozen SKUs across roll-on and spray, split across five fragrance families. Buying "a Nivea Men deodorant" without picking the family is how people end up with Cool Kick expecting Deep performance.

This file ranks the **families and variants** inside Nivea Men.

## The tier list

Ratings are FemaleDaily member scores; "rec" is the share of reviewers who recommend the product.

### S — Buy this

| Variant | Score | Why it earns S | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Deep Darkwood** | **4.7/5**, 100% rec (3 reviews) | The strongest *sweat* testimonial in the range: a reviewer who worked outdoors lifting goods called it "the driest of any OTC deodorant I've tried" | Tiny sample — 3 reviews |
| **Deep Charcoal Espresso** | **4.4/5**, 85% rec (14 reviews) | Best evidence-to-rating ratio in the range: absorbs fast, no white/yellow marks, no darkening, lasts a full day | Coffee scent is polarising; one reviewer notes it washes off in the shower so timing matters |

The mechanism behind the Deep family's advantage is visible in the reviews: both are charcoal/antiperspirant formulations, and reviewers describe **applying at night after a shower** so the antiperspirant works while sweat production is low — then it holds through the next day. One reviewer states this as a tip explicitly ([FemaleDaily](https://reviews.femaledaily.com/products/men-s-care/men-s-body-care/nivea/men-roll-on-deodorant-deep-darkwood)).

### A — Very good at one thing

| Variant | Score | Why it earns A | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Black & White Invisible Original** | **4.8/5**, 100% rec (11 reviews) | Highest score in the range; the anti-stain claim is genuinely verified by reviewers, on both white and black shirts | Reviewer consensus is "safe, no stains, decent scent" — nobody calls the sweat control exceptional |
| **Cool Kick Freezy Green** | **5.0/5**, 100% rec (7 reviews) | Fastest drying of the roll-ons — reviewers use it as the emergency option when running late | Perfect score on 7 reviews is not evidence; one reviewer notes it leaves marks on clothes |

### B — Popular, but delivers less than its reputation

| Variant | Score | Why it earns B | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cool Kick Cool Blue** | **4.1/5**, 81% rec (22 reviews) | The most-reviewed variant and the one stocked most widely; the cold hit on application is real and liked | The complaint repeats across negative reviews: **cooling yes, sweat control no** — "baru dipakai beraktivitas dikit ketek berasa basah" and "daya nahan keringatnya kurang nampol" |
| **Fresh Active** | **5.0/5** (1 review) | Described as soft-scented but lasting, non-sticky, fast-drying | One review; effectively unrated |

### Not rated

Silver Protect, Dry Impact, Epic Night, Deep Extra Dry, and the Derma Control pair have no meaningful FemaleDaily review volume. Silver Protect is nonetheless the variant most often named by men in Indonesian forum discussion for sweat control ([deodorant-men-indomaret-tier-list.md](./deodorant-men-indomaret-tier-list.md)) — so it sits outside this ranking rather than at the bottom of it.

## Read the ratings carefully

This is the most important caveat in the file, and it is specific to this dataset:

- **Most reviewers are women rating a men's product.** On Cool Kick Cool Blue, of 22 reviewers, the negative reviews are largely from women testing it or reporting on a partner. That is useful for *scent* and *texture*, weaker for *sweat control in a man's physiology*.
- **Deep Darkwood's 4.7 rests on 3 reviews.** Its single strongest testimonial is from a woman reporting her own use of a men's product. Treat 4.7 as "promising," not "proven."
- **Freezy Green's 5.0 rests on 7 reviews**, all recommending. Sampling of this size cannot distinguish a good product from a lucky one.
- **Cool Kick Cool Blue is the only variant with enough reviews to be reliable** (22), and it is also the lowest-rated. That is the most trustworthy single data point on this page.

Where a rating and a review count disagree, **weight the count.**

## Pick by situation

| If you… | Buy | Because |
|---|---|---|
| Sweat heavily, want max dryness | **Deep Darkwood** or **Deep Charcoal Espresso** | Only family whose reviewers report actual sweat reduction |
| Wear white or black shirts daily | **Black & White Invisible Original** | The anti-stain claim is the one reviewers verify |
| Run late in the morning | **Cool Kick Freezy Green** | Fastest-drying roll-on in the range |
| Want a cold hit on application | **Cool Kick Cool Blue** | Cooling is the one thing nobody disputes — but don't expect heavy sweat control |
| Work indoors, light activity | **Fresh Active** | Soft scent, non-sticky |
| Sensitive skin / just shaved | **Derma Control Defend** | Positioned for it; unrated here |
| Have no strong preference | **Deep Charcoal Espresso** | Best combination of rating (4.4) and review volume (14) |

## Practical notes from the reviews

- **Apply at night.** The Deep reviewers converge on this, and it is the mechanism that makes the 72 h family claim plausible in Indonesia.
- **The glass bottle is a shared weakness.** Multiple reviewers across variants flag that roll-on bottles shatter when dropped.
- **Stop reapplying during the day.** One reviewer notes that wiping or re-wetting lifts the antiperspirant layer.
- **"72 jam" is not tested by anyone.** Reviewers report a normal working day. See the reality check in the [men's Indomaret file](./deodorant-men-indomaret-tier-list.md).

## Open Questions

- **No male-controlled comparison exists.** The ratings are member scores from a predominantly female review community.
- **Silver Protect is the gap.** Indonesian men cite it repeatedly for sweat control, but it has no review volume on FemaleDaily, so it cannot be ranked against Deep.
- **Deep Darkwood vs Deep Espresso is not settled** — 4.7 on 3 reviews against 4.4 on 14 is a volume difference, not a quality difference.
- **Variant availability at a given Indomaret store is unmeasured.** Klik Indomaret's catalogue is wider than a single shop's shelf.
