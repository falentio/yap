# Gambler × Gamble!! (2024 remake) — Gameplay

> **2024 remake only.** This file documents **Gambler × Gamble!!** — note **two exclamation marks** — released by **Group SNE** on **2024-12-13**. It is a **remake/rebalance**, not a new design. For the 2016 original (one exclamation mark), see [Gambler × Gamble! (2016) — Gameplay](./gambler-x-gamble-2016-gameplay.md).

## TL;DR

- **Gambler × Gamble!!** (ギャンブラー×ギャンブル！！) is a **2024 remake** by the same designer, **Kaya Miyano** (宮野華也), art by **Geco Hirasawa** (平沢下戸), published by **Group SNE** on **2024-12-13**. It is the "**nine-years-in-the-making**" rework of the 2016 award-winning original ([Group SNE 2024 page](https://www.groupsne.co.jp/products/bg/gamble/index.html), [sne-ec](https://sne-ec.com/2024/12/13/b001-11/)).
- **Same core loop, bigger box, swingier play.** 3–4 players, 15–30 min, now **12+**. The publisher's pitch is "**coins flying back and forth**" with tension to the very end; the card count rose from **40 → 62** and chips from **28 → 32** ([Group SNE 2024 page](https://www.groupsne.co.jp/products/bg/gamble/index.html)).
- **Three named rule changes:** (1) **more opportunities to hire gamblers** during a round; (2) new **"special gamblers" that can only be purchased when you have 0 money** (a catch-up mechanic); (3) general rebalancing for flashier swings ([Group SNE 2024 page](https://www.groupsne.co.jp/products/bg/gamble/index.html), [Tabletop Simulator description](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3414848736)).
- **Reported win target:** one English reviewer states the remake ends at **$20 (20 chips)** rather than the original's 15 ([Hiew's Boardgame Blog, 2026-08-07](https://hiewandboardgames.blogspot.com/2026/08/gambler-x-gamble.html)). The publisher page omits a number, so this is **single-source and unverified**.
- **Confidence: low.** The publisher page and retailer listings give specs and a *thematic* summary of the changes, but **no public rulebook** exists for the remake; the precise new turn windows, the special-gambler cards, and the exact win threshold are **not documented in any source found**. Treat the "how it plays" below as **inherited from the 2016 loop plus the publisher's change list**, not as a verified remake rulebook.

## What is officially different from the 2016 original

The publisher's own summary names these adjustments ([Group SNE 2024 page](https://www.groupsne.co.jp/products/bg/gamble/index.html); mirrored by [sne-ec](https://sne-ec.com/2024/12/13/b001-11/), [Bic Camera](https://www.biccamera.com/bc/item/13705321/), and the [Tabletop Simulator mod](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3414848736)):

1. **More hiring opportunities.** "ギャンブラーを雇うタイミングが増えたり" — there are now **more moments in the round at which you can hire a gambler**, where the original allowed only the start player to hire (or enter Lucky Mode) once per round, at the end.
2. **Special gamblers buyable only at 0 money.** "所持金0の時のみ購入できる特別なギャンブラーが追加されたり" — a new class of **special gambler cards that can only be purchased when your money is exactly 0**, i.e. a designed **comeback mechanism** for a player who has been cleaned out.
3. **Rebalanced for flashier swings.** "大量のコインが行きかい最後の最後まで緊張が走る派手なプレイ感" — chips move in larger amounts and the game is tuned to stay tense to the end.

**Structural changes implied by the numbers:** the box grew by **22 cards** and **4 chips**, so the remake likely adds new gambler numbers and/or duplicates, and larger money denominations. The specific new cards are **not documented** in any source found.

## How it plays (core loop, inherited from 2016)

The remake is explicitly a rework of the same game, and the one English reviewer of the remake describes the identical spine ([Hiew's Boardgame Blog](https://hiewandboardgames.blogspot.com/2026/08/gambler-x-gamble.html)). As in 2016:

1. **Setup:** you start with money and **choose two different starting gambler cards**; you hold the Destiny number cards ("0" and "1" to begin).
2. **Each round, every player contributes one Destiny card to a shared sum.** The **start player plays face-up**; everyone else plays **face-down** and may **lie** about what they played.
3. **After all cards are committed, the start player may nudge the sum by ±1** (one chance).
4. **Reveal and total.** Any gambler whose **target number equals the sum** pays out to its owner(s) — the sum is the dice, players are the dice.
5. **Money buys more gamblers** (widening your range of winning numbers) or is spent on **Lucky Mode** (flip a gambler to its stronger side; it resets after a hit).
6. **Instant-win gamblers (No.4 / No.8)** exist: recruit them, and a hit wins the game outright ([Hiew's Boardgame Blog](https://hiewandboardgames.blogspot.com/2026/08/gambler-x-gamble.html)).

**Remake-specific notes from that review** ([Hiew](https://hiewandboardgames.blogspot.com/2026/08/gambler-x-gamble.html)):

- The win target is reported as **$20 / 20 chips** (vs 15 in the original). *[unverified]*
- Recruiting some gamblers **grants extra number cards** (the **−1, 2, or 3** Destiny cards) — same idea as the original's GET-2/GET-3 gamblers.
- Buying a big team **slows you down** toward the money goal (return on investment matters); upgrading a few gamblers can be better than hiring many.
- **Negotiation and deception are central:** if someone buys an instant-win gambler, everyone else nervously cooperates to avoid the sums of 4 or 8.
- Described as "**Machi Koro without dice**": instead of rolling to decide which cards earn, players collectively play the number.

## Win conditions (as reported)

- **Chip race:** reach the money target first — **reported 20 chips** in the remake ([Hiew](https://hiewandboardgames.blogspot.com/2026/08/gambler-x-gamble.html)). The original's target was 15. [unverified]
- **Instant-win gamblers:** the **No.4 / No.8** line from the original is retained in the review's description ([Hiew](https://hiewandboardgames.blogspot.com/2026/08/gambler-x-gamble.html)).
- Whether the original's **tie-break** (fewest gambler cards) and the **Gambler Fumble** are unchanged in the remake is **not documented**.

## Reception

- The remake is a **low-complexity, light filler** aimed at the same audience; the publisher positions it as a flashier version of a proven design, and notes the designer's *TRIO (Nana)* won a French game award in 2024 ([Group SNE 2024 page](https://www.groupsne.co.jp/products/bg/gamble/index.html)).
- The single detailed English play report is **cautiously positive but incomplete** — the reviewer played one rushed game and wanted to replay to explore the strategy space ([Hiew](https://hiewandboardgames.blogspot.com/2026/08/gambler-x-gamble.html)).
