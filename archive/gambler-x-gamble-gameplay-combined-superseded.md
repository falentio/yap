# Gambler × Gamble! / Gambler × Gamble!! — Gameplay

## TL;DR

- **Gambler × Gamble!** (ギャンブラー×ギャンブル！, *Gyanburā × Gyanburu!*) is a small Japanese **bluffing / simultaneous-selection card game** by **Kaya Miyano** (宮野華也) with art by **Geco Hirasawa** (平沢下戸), published by **cosaic / Group SNE** in **2016**. It won the first **Group SNE Board/Card Game Contest (2015)**. A **9-years-later remake, Gambler × Gamble!!** (two exclamation marks), shipped **2024-12-13** ([Group SNE product page](https://www.groupsne.co.jp/products/bg/gamble/gamble_howto.html), [2024 remake page](https://www.groupsne.co.jp/products/bg/gamble/index.html), [cosaic](http://www.cosaic.co.jp/games/gg.html)).
- **3–4 players, 15–30 min, light weight (BGG complexity ≈1.3).** You **hire "gamblers"** (cards, each with a target number 1–9, a cost, and a reward), then each round **everyone secretly contributes a "Destiny" number card**; the sum of all played numbers decides which gamblers pay out. It plays like **"Machi Koro without dice"** — the players themselves are the dice ([Hiew's Boardgame Blog](https://hiewandboardgames.blogspot.com/2026/08/gambler-x-gamble.html)).
- **Two win conditions (2016 edition):** be first to **15 money chips**, *or* have your **No.4 gambler** be a winning gambler (its Lucky-Mode face is **No.8**), an instant-win lottery card ([Group SNE how-to](https://www.groupsne.co.jp/products/bg/gamble/gamble_howto.html), [bodoge-tokyo](https://bodoge-tokyo.com/games/gambler-gamble)).
- **The twist that defines the game is the "Gambler Fumble":** if *nobody* wins a round, the richest player(s) must **surrender half their chips** to a pot that is later redistributed to everyone — so hoarding is punished ([Group SNE how-to](https://www.groupsne.co.jp/products/bg/gamble/gamble_howto.html), [uramako](https://uramako2.jugem.jp/?eid=739)).
- **Confidence: medium.** The 2016 loop is corroborated by the publisher's own tutorial page plus several independent Japanese rule write-ups. The **2024 remake's exact rule deltas** (extra hiring windows, "special gamblers" buyable only at 0 money, a **$20/20-chip** win target) are attested by the publisher blurb and one English reviewer but **not** by a public rulebook, so those specifics are flagged below.

## Context

**Disambiguation:** several unrelated games share the words "Gambler"/"Gamble" (e.g. the 1977 Parker Brothers *Gambler* roll-and-move board game; *Take a Gamble*). This file is **only** about the Japanese card game **Gambler × Gamble!** and its 2024 remake.

### Components (2016, verbatim breakdown)

- **Destiny cards (運命カード) — 14:** "0", "1", "2" ×4 each; "3" ×2.
- **Gambler cards (ギャンブラーカード) — 24:** No.1 / No.2 / No.3 ×5 each; No.4/8 ×1; No.5 / No.6 / No.7 / No.9 ×2 each. Each card has a **target number** (top-right), a **reward** (bottom-left), a **cost** (bottom-right), and possibly a **GET icon**.
- **Start-player card ×1**, **Fumble-stock card (ファンブルストック) ×1**.
- **Money chips ×28:** value-1 ×20, value-5 ×8.
- 1 rulebook.

([boardgamespeednews](https://boardgamespeednews.seesaa.net/article/449549456.html), [uramako](https://uramako2.jugem.jp/?eid=739))

## Setup

1. **Sort** gambler cards by target number; place them as a supply. Put the money chips and the "2"/"3" Destiny cards in the middle.
2. **Deal each player Destiny "0" and "1"** — their starting hand. (Destiny cards return to hand each round, so a hand is a small reusable set, not a deck.)
3. **Starting money by seat:** 1st player 1 chip, 2nd player 1 chip, 3rd player 2 chips, 4th player 2 chips ([uramako](https://uramako2.jugem.jp/?eid=739)). *(Some summaries simplify this to "two coins each" — see Open Questions.)*
4. **Draft starting gamblers:** each player takes **2 gambler cards from No.1–3**, free, chosen in seat order (snake draft); at this point only, **duplicate numbers are not allowed** ([Group SNE how-to](https://www.groupsne.co.jp/products/bg/gamble/gamble_howto.html), [uramako](https://uramako2.jugem.jp/?eid=739)).
5. Determine the start player (e.g. whoever "looks most like a gambler", or janken).

## The round loop

A game runs a number of rounds; the start player rotates clockwise each round. Each round has six steps:

1. **Start player plays a Destiny card face-up.**
2. **Everyone else plays one Destiny card face-down.** Players may freely *say* what they played; showing it is not allowed ([Group SNE how-to](https://www.groupsne.co.jp/products/bg/gamble/gamble_howto.html), [uramako](https://uramako2.jugem.jp/?eid=739)).
3. **Start player may place 1 chip on the ±1 card** (its +1 or −1 face). This is a **start-player-only privilege**. Paying 1 chip is the cost; the chip goes to the supply. If used, the total **must** be adjusted by +1 or −1. Declining is allowed. Only one chip, once ([Group SNE how-to](https://www.groupsne.co.jp/products/bg/gamble/gamble_howto.html), [uramako](https://uramako2.jugem.jp/?eid=739)).
4. **Reveal all Destiny cards and total the numbers** (then apply the ±1 if paid). Every gambler whose **target number equals the total** is a **"winning gambler" (当たり)**. **Every** player owning a matching gambler — including duplicates — collects that card's **reward** in chips. Lucky-Mode gamblers pay more ([handman](http://handmanboardgame.com/ボードゲーム紹介/ギャンブラー✕ギャンブル！/), [Group SNE how-to](https://www.groupsne.co.jp/products/bg/gamble/gamble_howto.html)).
5. **Start player only: hire a gambler OR enter Lucky Mode** (see below) — exactly one of the two.
6. **Cleanup:** return played Destiny cards to hands; pass the start-player card clockwise.

([handman](http://handmanboardgame.com/ボードゲーム紹介/ギャンブラー✕ギャンブル！/), [uramako](https://uramako2.jugem.jp/?eid=739))

### The Gambler Fumble (ギャンブルファンブル)

If a round produces **no winning gambler at all**, the **player(s) with the most chips** must move **half their chips (rounded down)** onto the **Fumble-stock card**. As soon as the stock holds **at least as many chips as there are players**, it is **split evenly among all players**; any remainder stays on the card for next time ([Group SNE how-to](https://www.groupsne.co.jp/products/bg/gamble/gamble_howto.html), [uramako](https://uramako2.jugem.jp/?eid=739)).

This is the game's central balancing mechanism: a "bust" round takes money from whoever is ahead and sprinkles it over the table, so **sitting on a big pile is dangerous** and players are incentivised to keep sums landing on *someone's* number ([deepdepths](https://deepdepths.doorblog.jp/archives/14078908.html)).

## Hiring and Lucky Mode (the two economy levers)

At step 5 the start player does exactly one of:

- **Hire a gambler.** Pay the card's **cost** (bottom-right number) and place it face-up in front of you. Some cards carry a **"GET 2" / "GET 3"** icon: hiring them **also grants you the "2" or "3" Destiny card** (usable from the next round). Duplicate gambler numbers can be hired freely, but each of the "2"/"3" Destiny cards can be obtained **only once per player** ([uramako](https://uramako2.jugem.jp/?eid=739), [deepdepths](https://deepdepths.doorblog.jp/archives/14078908.html)).
- **Enter Lucky Mode (豪運モード).** Flip **all** of your gamblers of **one chosen number** to their reverse side, **free**. Lucky gamblers pay **more** when they hit, but a Lucky gambler **reverts to normal after it wins once** ([Group SNE how-to](https://www.groupsne.co.jp/products/bg/gamble/gamble_howto.html), [handman](http://handmanboardgame.com/ボードゲーム紹介/ギャンブラー✕ギャンブル！/)).

**Cost / reward economics (2016):**

| Gambler | Cost | Grants | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| No.1, No.2, No.3 | 1 | — | Starting pool; cheap, low reward |
| No.5, No.6, No.7 | 3 | Destiny "2" | Mid-tier |
| No.9 | 3 | Destiny "3" | High number, hard to reach in 3-player |
| **No.4 / No.8** | **10** | — | **Instant-win lottery card** (No.4 normal, No.8 Lucky) |

([deepdepths](https://deepdepths.doorblog.jp/archives/14078908.html), [uramako](https://uramako2.jugem.jp/?eid=739))

Because higher-numbered gamblers pay more, the usual arc is to **buy bigger numbers as the Destiny pool grows**; hiring duplicates of a number you already own multiplies that number's payout ([chokobodo](https://chokobodo.com/gambler-x-gamble/), [deepdepths](https://deepdepths.doorblog.jp/archives/14078908.html)).

## Win conditions

The game ends the moment any of the following is true:

1. **Chip race:** a player holds **15 or more chips** ([bodoge-tokyo](https://bodoge-tokyo.com/games/gambler-gamble), [uramako](https://uramako2.jugem.jp/?eid=739)).
2. **The No.4 jackpot:** a player owning the **No.4 gambler** has it be a **winning gambler** — they win instantly. (If that player has the No.4 card flipped to its **Lucky (No.8)** face, they win on a hit of **8** instead of 4.) The No.4/8 card is **unique (1 copy)**, so it is first-come, first-served ([Group SNE how-to](https://www.groupsne.co.jp/products/bg/gamble/gamble_howto.html), [bodoge-tokyo](https://bodoge-tokyo.com/games/gambler-gamble)).

**Ties:** if chip totals are equal, the player holding **fewer gambler cards** wins; if still tied, the victory is shared ([uramako](https://uramako2.jugem.jp/?eid=739)).

## The 2024 remake — what changed

**Gambler × Gamble!!** (two exclamation marks) is a **9-year-in-the-making remake** by the same designer, with **new art direction and rebalanced contents** (62 cards vs 40; 32 chips vs 28) ([Group SNE 2024 page](https://www.groupsne.co.jp/products/bg/gamble/index.html), [sne-ec](https://sne-ec.com/2024/12/13/b001-11/)). The publisher's own summary of the changes:

- **More opportunities to hire gamblers** (extra hiring windows during the round).
- **New "special gamblers" that can only be purchased when you have 0 money** — a catch-up / comeback mechanic.
- **Flashier, swingier play** — "large amounts of coins flying back and forth" and tension to the very end ([Group SNE 2024 page](https://www.groupsne.co.jp/products/bg/gamble/index.html), [Tabletop Simulator description](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3414848736)).

**Win target change:** one English reviewer of the remake states the game now ends at **$20 (20 chips)** rather than 15 ([Hiew's Boardgame Blog, 2026-08-07](https://hiewandboardgames.blogspot.com/2026/08/gambler-x-gamble.html)). The publisher's page does **not** state a numeric target, so treat "20" as **single-source / probable, not confirmed** [unverified]. The remake also raises the **age rating to 12+**.

Core loop unchanged: pick/keep two different starting gamblers, everyone secretly plays a Destiny number, start player can nudge the sum by ±1, matching gamblers pay out, Lucky Mode doubles down, and the Fumble punishes the chip leader ([Hiew's Boardgame Blog](https://hiewandboardgames.blogspot.com/2026/08/gambler-x-gamble.html)).

## Strategy and player interaction

- **The ±1 is decisive.** Many rounds hinge on whether the start player nudges the sum onto or off a target; the cost of 1 chip is trivial next to what it changes ([chokobodo](https://chokobodo.com/gambler-x-gamble/), [bodoge-hoobby review](https://bodoge.hoobby.net/games/gambler-x-gamble/reviews/396)).
- **Table talk is the game.** Because only the start player plays face-up, everyone else's card is a claim. Lying about what you played is explicitly allowed, so reading opponents' incentives (which numbers *they* own) matters more than the cards ([Group SNE how-to](https://www.groupsne.co.jp/products/bg/gamble/gamble_howto.html), [Hiew](https://hiewandboardgames.blogspot.com/2026/08/gambler-x-gamble.html)).
- **Collusion and sabotage are legal.** Players may coordinate to deliberately produce a bust and force a Fumble that drains the chip leader ([handman](http://handmanboardgame.com/ボードゲーム紹介/ギャンブラー✕ギャンブル！/)).
- **Wide vs. tall:** buying many different numbers widens your hit range (steady income); stacking duplicates of one number and flipping it to Lucky Mode is the high-variance "one big score" line ([chokobodo](https://chokobodo.com/gambler-x-gamble/), [deepdepths](https://deepdepths.doorblog.jp/archives/14078908.html)).
- **No.9 is nearly dead in 3-player** — reaching a sum of 9 requires most players to play high cards *and* the start player to add +1, so reviewers call it close to unplayable at that count ([bodoge-hoobby review](https://bodoge.hoobby.net/games/gambler-x-gamble), [chokobodo](https://chokobodo.com/gambler-x-gamble/)).
- **No.4 is a trap-or-jackpot:** spending 10 chips to buy it delays the 15-chip race badly; reviewers often conclude it is better to just keep earning ([deepdepths](https://deepdepths.doorblog.jp/archives/14078908.html), [chokobodo](https://chokobodo.com/gambler-x-gamble/)).

## Reception

- **BGG ratings conflict by snapshot.** A 2017-03 snapshot lists **7.2/10** ([handman](http://handmanboardgame.com/ボードゲーム紹介/ギャンブラー✕ギャンブル！/)); a later aggregator shows **5.6/10, rank ≈11,700, weight 1.33** ([boardgamesforallages](https://boardgamesforallages.com/game/gambler-x-gamble/)). Treat both as point-in-time and **not directly comparable**.
- **Play-feel votes (bodoge-hoobby)** skew heavily to **psychology/bluff (13)** and **strategy (11)** over **luck/probability (3)**, i.e. players read it as a reading game, not a dice game ([bodoge-hoobby](https://bodoge.hoobby.net/games/gambler-x-gamble)).
- **Common praise:** quick, cheap, elegant "reading" filler; nice art; the Lucky-Mode art flip is a favourite detail. **Common criticism:** "a bit coarse/large-grained", 3-player balance issues, and the No.4 cost being questionable ([bodoge-hoobby reviews](https://bodoge.hoobby.net/games/gambler-x-gamble), [chokobodo](https://chokobodo.com/gambler-x-gamble/)).
- **Lineage:** reviewers describe it as a **"thumb-guessing" ("いっせーの") game in card form** and as **"Machi Koro without dice"** — the collective sum replaces the dice roll ([deepdepths](https://deepdepths.doorblog.jp/archives/14078908.html), [Hiew](https://hiewandboardgames.blogspot.com/2026/08/gambler-x-gamble.html)).

## Open Questions

- **2024 remake win target.** "20 chips" rests on a single English review ([Hiew](https://hiewandboardgames.blogspot.com/2026/08/gambler-x-gamble.html)); the publisher page omits it. Needs the remake rulebook or a Japanese review that states the number. [unverified]
- **Exact remake rule deltas.** "More hiring timings" and "special gamblers buyable only at 0 money" are from the publisher blurb only; the *number*, *costs*, and *effects* of the special gamblers are unknown.
- **Starting chips.** Official summary says "two coins"; a detailed Japanese walkthrough gives a seat-based 1/1/2/2 split ([uramako](https://uramako2.jugem.jp/?eid=739)). Which is correct for the shipped 2016 rules is unresolved.
- **±1 chip destination.** One source says the paid chip goes to the **supply**; whether the Fumble stock or bank is meant is not fully pinned down.
- **Full gambler card table.** Per-card target/cost/reward/lucky-reward values were not captured (cards were not readable in the sources). Costs 1/3/10 and the GET-2/GET-3 rules are attested; the rest of the table is not.
- **Lucky Mode payout multiplier.** Confirmed to be "higher", but the exact numbers are unknown.
- **English rulebook.** No official English rules PDF found; English coverage is secondary/derivative.
- **BGG pages bot-blocked.** Both BGG entries ([197071](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/197071/gambler-gamble), [436522](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/436522/gambler-gamble)) returned `bot_blocked` on 2026-10-11; rating/rank/mechanic tags not captured directly.
