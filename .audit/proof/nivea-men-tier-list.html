The short version

  • S: Deep Darkwood and Deep Charcoal Espresso. The only family whose reviews are about sweat control actually working — 4.7 and 4.4.
  • A: Black & White Invisible Original, 4.8/5. The anti-stain specialist, and the one variant where reviewers verify the clothes claim.
  • B: Cool Kick. The most-bought variant and the lowest-rated here — 4.1/5 over 22 reviews, the most trustworthy number on this page. Complaint repeats: cooling yes, sweat control no.

The tier list

SBuy this
  • Deep Darkwood 4.7 · 3 reviews — the strongest sweat testimonial in the range; a reviewer doing outdoor lifting work called it the driest OTC deodorant she'd used. Watch: 3 reviews is a promise, not proof.
  • Deep Charcoal Espresso 4.4 · 14 reviews — best evidence-to-rating ratio: absorbs fast, no white or yellow marks, no darkening, holds a full day. Watch: coffee scent polarises; washes off in the shower.
AVery good at one thing
  • Black & White Invisible Original 4.8 · 11 reviews — highest score in the range; anti-stain genuinely verified on both white and black shirts. Watch: nobody calls the sweat control exceptional.
  • Cool Kick Freezy Green 5.0 · 7 reviews — fastest-drying roll-on; the emergency option when running late. Watch: one reviewer notes it marks clothes.
BPopular, delivers less than its reputation
  • Cool Kick Cool Blue 4.1 · 22 reviews — most-stocked and most-bought; the cold hit on application is real and liked. Watch: the negative reviews converge — "baru dipakai beraktivitas dikit ketek berasa basah".
  • Fresh Active 5.0 · 1 review — soft-scented but lasting, non-sticky, fast-drying. Watch: effectively unrated.
—Not rated
  • Silver Protect · Dry Impact · Epic Night · Deep Extra Dry · Derma Control — no meaningful review volume. Silver Protect is the variant most often named by Indonesian men for sweat control, so it sits outside this ranking rather than at its bottom.

Read the ratings carefully

This caveat is specific to this dataset, and it changes how you should use the page.

  • Most reviewers are women rating a men's product. On Cool Kick Cool Blue, the negative reviews are largely women testing it or reporting on a partner. Good for scent and texture; weaker for sweat control in a man's physiology.
  • Deep Darkwood's 4.7 rests on 3 reviews — and its strongest testimonial is a woman reporting her own use.
  • Cool Kick Cool Blue is the only variant with reliable volume (22) — and it's also the lowest-rated. That's the most trustworthy single data point here.
