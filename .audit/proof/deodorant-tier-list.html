Deodorant at Indomaret — Tier List

Thirteen brands on the shelf, ranked by what Indonesian reviewers report — not by what the packaging claims.

The short version

  • Vaseline Dry Serum is the only 9/10 in a head-to-head that also covered Rexona, Nivea and Hygr.
  • Nivea is the safest blind buy — nobody rated it below 4/5 except one outlier.
  • Rexona contradicts itself. #1 seller at the till, rated 0/10 by a reviewer whose shirts it stained.
  • MBK powder wins rupiah-per-result for heavy sweaters — the only thing that worked after roll-ons failed.
  • The "mencerahkan" brightening claim fails or takes weeks. Buy it as an antiperspirant, not a treatment.

The tier list

SReviewers keep buying it again
  • Vaseline Dry Serum 20–30k — 9/10; brightens, doesn't stain, lasts. Watch: absorbs slowly for some.
  • Rexona lotion sachets ~3k — 4.6/5, top of a sachet round-up; non-alcohol, halal. Watch: constant re-buy.
  • Marina Fresh Sparkle 12–20k — multi-repurchase; 6–8 h real scent in AC, no stains. Watch: not for heavy sweaters.
AReliable, with one named trade-off
  • Nivea roll-on 23–25k — 4.6/5 for all-day scent and packaging. Watch: glass bottle shatters on a drop.
  • Posh Hijab ~17.5k — the Indomaret cashier's own pick; creams its 48 h claim. Watch: reads sweet for some.
  • Dove serum 27.5–49.5k — 4.5/5, highest of the brighteners; soft scent, fast absorption. Watch: dries skin over time.
  • Kahf 25–30k — 0% alcohol, built for sensitive skin. Watch: rests on halal appeal, not review volume.
BWorks for some, fails for others
  • Rexona roll-on 15–25k — the store's best seller by transaction count. Watch: one reviewer 0/10, darkened underarms and stains.
  • MBK powder 8–12k — 5/5; ended body odor where roll-ons failed, at the lowest price. Watch: mess, not travel-friendly.
  • Casablanca ~15k — lasting "expensive-smelling" fragrance; buyers cite no stain, no irritation. Watch: named in the brightening-failure complaint.
  • Pixy stick ~15k — 4/5 for softness and lasting scent. Watch: melts in heat.
CWorks as a fragrance, not as protection
  • Axe Dark Temptation · Adidas Ice Dive 30–46k — recommended on aroma alone; body spray has no sweat-reduction mechanism.
  • Lady Speed Stick ~63k — 24 h claim where 48–72 h is the baseline, at a premium price.
DFailed in reviews
  • Hygr 8–12k — 5/10: stained shirts, stopped working, darkened underarms.
  • Nivea, per one reviewer — 2/10, "same as Rexona but lasts a bit longer". Conflicts with the 4.6/5 above.

The brightening controversy

Every brand sells a "mencerahkan" variant. It is the most disputed claim in the corpus, and both camps describe the same thing at different magnitudes.

  • Where it works: a slow, partial effect. Two independent reviewers use near-identical words — "warna jadi lebih rata setelah beberapa minggu" and "bukan yang tiba-tiba putih banget".
  • Where it fails: one reviewer used Rexona, Nivea, Casablanca and Dove brighteners — "zonk semua, malah ketiak makin hitam."
  • On darkening: the most useful hypothesis here is friction, rough shaving and product-skin mismatch — not aluminium alone. Self-reported, not clinical.
